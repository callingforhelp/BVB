# Scene 47332005 — refinement findings

Bathroom + living-room scene. QA: toilet size 84cm, sofa size 103cm, room 15.6㎡,
sofa↔toilet 3.9m (object_abs_distance), route_planning (proposition).

## Issues encountered (and how they resolved)

1. **Direction confusion (the big early time-sink).** Root cause: the user's 2D plan has
   **X→left, Y→down**. Blender's default top view is X-right/Y-up. Fix: set the viewport to
   `view_rotation = Quaternion((0,0,0,1))` so screen-right = world −X and screen-up = world −Y.
   After that, "left"=+X, "right"=−X, "up"=−Y, "down"=+Y and the user's words map cleanly.

2. **room_area vs distance over-constraint.** room_area scores the floor **bbox** (capped at
   15.6 ±1.56 = 17.16㎡). Several layouts that satisfied 15.6 + toilet-centered + 3.9m were
   geometrically incompatible. Resolution evolved through: narrow-deep rectangle → two-box L →
   compact bathroom + bigger living room.

3. **The distance metric — fully reverse-engineered.** `closest_distance` grounds each
   `object_ref` to its **single LARGEST sub-part** (longest bbox edge) via `_match_group`, then
   computes the **3-D axis-aligned gap** between the two group bboxes, where each bbox is
   `obj.location ± obj.scale/2` (NOT `matrix_world`, NOT the mesh). **Gotcha:** `Sofa_Back`
   spans the body's full width, so it tied/beat `Sofa_Body` and got grounded as "sofa" — the
   distance was then measured from the **back** (the part nearest the toilet), collapsing it.
   My hand-measured matrix_world distances disagreed with the gate by ±0.5m repeatedly until I
   read the eval code. **Always replicate the grounding or run `gate`.**

4. **Toilet wall choice drives the distance.** With the toilet on the LEFT wall, the X-gap to
   the sofa is ~0.6m, so ~all of 3.9m must come from Y. "Move the toilet up" and "put the sofa
   back on the lower edge" both shrink that Y-gap. User chose to keep the video-faithful layout
   and accept the distance QA failing (`不改了，就这样吧`).

5. **Furniture iteration.** Many small sofa edits: shrink the sofa to make the room *feel*
   bigger (room_area is wall-capped, so you can't grow walls); chaise at the body's upper-left
   with its X kept *within* the body (no stick-out); backrest on the body's +Y edge (sets the
   facing); fix chaise/body 穿模 (overlap). Keep `Sofa_Body` ≥92.7cm so the size QA holds.

6. **Cleanup after reshapes.** Deepening the living room left the side walls short of the new
   back wall (gaps); an old `Door_Panel`+frames was orphaned floating mid-living-room; the
   vanity poked through the wall and the toilet was mirrored backwards after a 180° rotate.
   Lesson: after any structural reshape, scan for orphaned objects + walls that don't connect.
